What it does

The jobs that eat your office's week, done in minutes

Results

Upload the university's tabulation sheet. CGPA updates itself.

Drop in the result sheet as PDF or Excel. The portal reads every roll, grade and GPA, keeps only your department's students, and works out course credits from the printed GPAs.

  • Retakes and improvements count the best grade
  • Running CGPA and credits earned after every semester
  • Every correction is kept in a history with the reason

Finance

A fee ledger that can't charge a student twice

Every bill, payment, fine and correction is a line in the student's ledger. Balances add up from those lines, so nothing goes missing and nothing gets billed twice for the same semester.

  • Students pay monthly or all at once
  • Batch billing skips anyone already billed
  • Students see the same history on their phone

Teachers

Course teachers enter CT marks from a link in their email

The academic office sets up the semester's courses and teachers. Each teacher gets an email, opens their own course and fills in class test marks for their students. Three-credit courses get four CTs and two-credit courses get three.
