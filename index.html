Undergraduate thesis · KUET

Secure and Reliable Data Forwarding Against Blackhole and Wormhole Attacks in Mobile Ad Hoc Networks

A data forwarding scheme for MANETs that avoids both blackhole and wormhole attacks. It builds on the AOMDV multipath routing protocol and adds an enhanced homomorphic cryptosystem (Gorti) that supports both additive and multiplicative operations with keys of up to 2048 bits, protecting data integrity in transit.

  • AOMDV
  • Homomorphic encryption
  • NS-2.35 simulation
