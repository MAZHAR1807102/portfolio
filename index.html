Undergraduate thesis · KUET

Secure and Reliable Data Forwarding Against Blackhole and Wormhole Attacks in Mobile Ad Hoc Networks

A data forwarding scheme for MANETs that avoids both blackhole and wormhole attacks. It builds on the AOMDV multipath routing protocol and adds an enhanced homomorphic cryptosystem (Gorti) that supports both additive and multiplicative operations with keys of up to 2048 bits, protecting data integrity in transit.

  • AOMDV
  • Homomorphic encryption
  • NS-2.35 simulation

Department Management Portal

A web portal that runs a university-affiliated engineering department: fees, attendance, CT marks, results and exam paperwork in one place. The HOD, finance, academic and exam offices, teachers and students each have their own login and see only their own work.

  • Imports the university's result sheets (PDF or Excel), works out course credits from the printed GPAs, and keeps a running CGPA that counts the best attempt.
  • A fee ledger with batch billing, partial payments and attendance fines that never bills a student twice.
  • Generates exam roll sheets as Word files in the university's layout, with Bangla names converted from old Bijoy-font files.
  • Teachers enter CT marks from an emailed sign-in link; students see results, dues and attendance on their phone.
  • Access for each role is enforced in the database, and every result correction is kept in a history.
